## First review session

1. Select the first, middle, and final lecture in verified course order. For each, write one tangible candidate skill and find its original evidence spans. This is discovery, not the final course scope.
2. For each candidate, classify evidence as supported, needing original media, needing a supplement, or unsupported. Record the exact source revision, timecodes, required assets, and recovery effort.
3. Have the subject reviewer independently solve a proposed exercise and an unseen transfer variant. Decide whether the prerequisites, factual correction policy, and task difficulty fit the intended learners.
4. Freeze the full course's outcome matrix and required artifacts only after this discovery. Estimate actual input/output token volumes for the chosen prompts and provider; set numeric run/pilot caps before dispatch.
5. Prepare development/reference examples and a source-grouped untouched holdout. Every held-out video's variants and alternate playlist memberships remain in the same group.

Per-outcome record to complete:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Skill and learner | Observable task, prerequisites, and expected learner background. |
| Source support | Original video ID, revision/hash, segment IDs/timecodes, required audio/video/slides or approved supplemental references. |
| Source sufficiency | Supported status, or a named unresolved evidence gap with its resolution owner. |
| Practice and transfer | Worked example, independent practice, fresh unaided task, independent answer/rationale, and prewritten scoring criterion. |
| Corrections/exclusions | Original claim/segment, proposed treatment, evidence, and subject reviewer disposition. |
| Required artifacts | Lesson/node IDs, inline assessment, editable diagram and accessible alternative, wiki/graph references. |
