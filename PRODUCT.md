## Platform

web

## Stack

Cloudflare Pages is settled. Astro is the current recommendation; full-catalog artifact compatibility and capacity remain unproved. The existing Python factory supplies content; it is not the public application's runtime.

## Users

Arabic-speaking university learners studying subjects across higher-education specializations. Independent study covers explicitly stated outcomes and prerequisites; lab/supervision limits remain visible. Reviewers and the operator work privately before publication.

## Product Purpose

Help learners discover, understand and independently apply their subjects through clear courses, meaningful practice and connected concepts, without an account. Search and AI/chat citation eligibility are explicit discovery priorities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Destination: roughly 325 courses and thousands of lessons; these are planning targets, not accepted-content counts.
- Arabic-first, strong English/mixed-direction support, phone-first and low-bandwidth online. Offline course downloads are not a launch promise.
- Canonical Markdown, inline assessment, reviewed diagrams and media, precomputed simplified explanations, course/lesson wiki and cross-course typed concept graph.
- Support approved skills-output families through a defined publishing contract; no arbitrary authored HTML/JavaScript execution.
- No learner accounts for now. Browser-local progress is the implementation recommendation; it is not a mastery credential.
- Artifact hosting/operations cost at most $5/month total. Generation remains on the existing zIDE quota. Keep the local corpus and artifact JSON out of GitHub.
- Named review precedes operator approval; only eligible accepted bundles publish. No deployment or storage-capacity guarantee has yet been demonstrated.

## Brand Commitments

Fanout is the product-finish/navigation reference. The platform brief specifies Thmanyah, Arabic-first reading and one accent per course; font publishing rights remain a release gate. Product name/domain are not yet selected.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing docs and [wireframes](docs/wireframes/index.html); local private transcripts and preparation artifacts. The optics pilot is not an accepted public course. Do not invent learner counts, testimonials, endorsements or review evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful learning action clear.
- Preserve academic meaning and discipline-specific practice.
- Keep discovery open and the learner experience consistent across formats.
- Separate private production/review from public learning.
- Treat reviewed correctness, accessibility and the monthly ceiling as constraints on delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard/touch operation, readable Arabic/English/math, accessible visual alternatives, reduced-motion support and usable weak-phone layouts. Detailed browser/performance acceptance is still to be qualified.
